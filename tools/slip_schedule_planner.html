Schedule

What "With fixes" changes

  • The minimum gap is enforced after slip and catch blocks are shuffled, in the order they will run. The original checks it before the shuffle, and only when the previous slip was at 55 s or later.
  • It is measured between delivered onsets. The second slip in a block is armed no earlier than one minimum gap after the first slip is delivered.
  • Combinations are drawn in rounds, each containing every combination once, so every trial gets a near-equal share. The original balances only over the whole session.

The CSV and the shoe app

  • The app arms each slip at arm_in_trial_s, waits for the gait event and fires; it enforces the minimum gap again between the onsets that really happen, and retries a slip that could not be delivered.
  • sim_onset_in_trial_s and sim_gap_s are this page's delivery model, for checking the design only.
  • Blank direction / delay_ms use the YAML default for the slip type. Condition lists fill both in: anterior (+velocity) or posterior (−velocity), and ms after the gait event.
  • Type names the app accepts: Early/ES/HS, Mid/MS/FF, Late/LS/HO. The magnitude must be a peak speed in m/s.

Not shown here

  • Timing inside the stride (ES preparation time, LS at 40% of stride). The original drops the ES preparation time when it rolls forward a stride; that bug is too small to see at this scale.
  • Overground walking. Blocks here are continuous treadmill time; an overground version needs passes, turns and excluded acceleration steps.
  • Delivery delay is drawn uniformly from 0 to one stride as a stand-in for waiting for the target gait event.
